## SLIDE 05 — UNIQUE TECHNOLOGY

### What makes Kloud technically irreplaceable:

#### NDB Score — Cognitive Deviation Tracking
> Measures how far a node's execution pattern deviates from its expected behavioral envelope.  
> Low NDB (0.041) = stable, predictable, no anomalous compute.  
> NDB spike = something changed — catch it before it becomes an incident.

#### STIGMA — Behavioral Security Telemetry
```
L1 Minimal   → Routine low-friction operations
L2 Standard  → Normal operational events (current: 6/6)
L3 Compact   → Elevated, compressed, potentially anomalous
```
Every API request is classified, scored, and logged.  
No implicit trust. No blind spots.

#### TIDE — Fabric Tension Monitor
> Measures environmental and peer-driven pressure on the node.  
> Low TIDE = stable runtime. Rising TIDE = early warning system.

#### CRDT State Store — Conflict-Free Distributed State
> State replication without coordination.  
> Causal ordering. Mutation tracking. Audit-ready.

**These capabilities are native to Kloud and form our core moat.**
