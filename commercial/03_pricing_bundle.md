## OVERVIEW

**Kloud Sovereign Fabric Suite** is a bundled platform offering combining four service layers into a single subscription:

1. **Sovereign Node Deployment** — Private compute, your infrastructure, your rules
2. **Managed Fabric** — 24/7 ops, SLA, observability, incident response
3. **Security Posture Engine** — STIGMA + NDB behavioral telemetry
4. **API Compute Credits** — Usage-based distributed compute

All tiers include all four layers. Tiers are differentiated by scale, SLA level, support intensity, and customization.

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?**  
A: Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades apply at the next billing cycle.

**Q: What happens if I exceed my API ops limit?**  
A: Operations continue uninterrupted. Overage is billed at the rate for your tier.

**Q: Can I bring my own infrastructure (BYOI)?**  
A: Yes. Kloud nodes deploy on your Hetzner, AWS, GCP, Azure, or bare-metal servers.

**Q: Is the STIGMA/NDB data visible only to me?**  
A: Yes. All telemetry is local-first. Kloud does not have access to your node data unless explicitly granted for support purposes.

**Q: What is the minimum contract term?**  
A: Starter and Pro are month-to-month. Enterprise requires a minimum 12-month contract.

**Q: Do you support air-gapped / disconnected deployments?**  
A: Yes, on Enterprise tier. Requires custom setup via Professional Services.
